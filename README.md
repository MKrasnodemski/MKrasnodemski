## 💼 Featured BI & Automation Projects

### 📱 Quality Management System (QMS)
**Tech Stack:** AppSheet, Google Apps Script, Google Sheets

- **The Problem:** Inefficient, paper-based quality control processes on the warehouse floor.
- **The Solution:** I designed and developed a custom mobile application to digitize warehouse quality control operations. The app equips controllers with an interactive SKU catalog, real-time historical data, and automated alerts for past product defects.
- **The Impact:** Directly increased overall QC efficiency by 13% and eliminated manual data entry by automating report processing in the background.

### 📊 E-commerce Aftersales Dashboards
**Tech Stack:** Power BI, DAX, Data Modeling

- **The Problem:** The need for transparent monitoring of returns (RMA), material quality, and carrier performance in a dynamically growing branch.
- **The Solution:** Built interactive dashboards tracking the metrics that actually drive reverse-logistics decisions — Returns by Country, Transport Damage by Carrier, and Damaged Items by RMA Reason as a share of total return volume.
- **The Impact:** Enabled the management team to quickly identify logistical bottlenecks and make data-driven decisions regarding reverse logistics. *(Note: Data in the repository is anonymized.)*

**Tech Stack:** C#, .NET, WPF, Visual Studio, QuestPDF

- **The Solution:** I developed a custom desktop application as my engineering thesis to automate complex electrical calculations. The tool instantly assesses circuit designs for compliance with the strict PN-HD 60364 standard and generates formatted PDF reports.
- **The Impact:** Defended with the highest possible grade (5.0) and officially nominated for the Rector's Award for the Best Engineering Thesis of the 2025/2026 academic year.
